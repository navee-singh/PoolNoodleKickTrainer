Door Noodle Kick Target

A pool noodle sticks straight out of a closed interior door at any height you want. Six printed parts plus two bungee cords: hangers clip over the top and under the bottom of the door, the cords run down the face, and the noodle mount grips the cords wherever you set it.

How it works

Hangers (×4)

A thin C-clip hooks over the top edge of the door, and the same part flipped hooks under the bottom edge. Each has a thin 10 mm loop at one edge for the bungee hook. It stands off the door face so the hook never touches paint.

Bungees (×2 runs)

Each run goes from a top hanger, straight down the door face, to the bottom hanger below it. Two runs 144 mm apart. Chain two cords hook-to-hook if one isn't long enough; the joint can sit anywhere.

Noodle mount

A backplate with a deep socket for the noodle, a spigot that goes into the noodle's hollow core, and a cross pin. On each side, three capped pegs grip a cord as it weaves left-right-left, so the whole mount holds wherever you put it.

Mount the target on the side the door swings toward. Kicks then push the door into its stop instead of trying to pull it open, and the thin back leg of each hanger sits on the stop side where the gap is tightest.

Measure first

Three numbers decide the fit. Change them at the top of build.py and rerun it if yours differ.

Print settings

  • Material: PETG preferred. It flexes instead of snapping, which matters for the hangers. PLA works for the mount.
  • Hangers: 100% infill (they're small), 0.2 mm layers. These carry all the cord tension.
  • Mount: 4 walls, 25 to 30% gyroid infill, 5 top and bottom layers.
  • Pin: 100% infill.
  • Supports: none needed for any part.
  • Everything fits on one 256 × 256 bed (see the Print bed view).

You also need

  • The pool noodle. Cut it to about 0.8 to 1 m; full length droops.
  • Bungee cords: enough to span the door twice, top to bottom (about 2 m per side). Two 1 m cords chained per side is typical.
  • Optional: felt pads or painter's tape on the back of the mount and inside the hangers to protect the finish.

Install

  1. Hang the top hangers

    Open the door. Hook two hangers over the top edge, about 144 mm apart (centered on where you want the noodle). The thick leg with the eye goes on the target side, the thin leg on the stop side.

  2. Place the bottom hangers

    Flip the other two upside down and slide them under the bottom edge, directly below the top ones. Eyes on the target side again.

  3. Run the cords

    Hold the hook with its curve flat against the door and slip the tip sideways through the loop, from the open side. Run the cord down the face and hook the bottom eye, chaining cords as needed. Aim for a firm stretch, about 15 to 25% longer than relaxed. Repeat on the other side.

  4. Weave the mount onto the cords

    Hold the mount at the height you want. On each side, press the cord between the pegs so it passes left of the top peg, right of the middle peg, left of the bottom peg. The cord tucks under the caps. For more grip, take a full wrap around the middle peg.

  5. Seat the noodle

    Twist the noodle into the socket until it bottoms out over the spigot. Push the cross pin through the side holes and through the foam.

  6. Close the door and test

    Close and latch the door. Kick lightly first and check that the hangers stay seated and the mount holds its height.

Changing height

Lift the cord off the pegs on both sides, slide the mount up or down, and weave it back on. No hardware, under 30 seconds. Useful range is about 20 cm off the floor to 1.85 m.

If the mount creeps down under hard kicks, add the extra wrap around the middle peg on both sides, or tighten the cords. Grip rises with cord tension.

Watch out for

  • Door won't latch: the stop molding is pinching the thin back leg. Reduce H_BACK_T to 2.0 and reprint, or move the hangers to where the gap is looser.
  • Hollow-core doors are fine since the load goes into the door edges, not the skin.
  • Solid noodle (no core): set NOODLE_CORE = False and rely on the socket ribs and pin.
  • Kick the noodle, not the mount. The socket is 9 cm deep to keep feet away from the hard parts.

Regenerating the files

All geometry comes from build.py. Edit the parameter block, then run pip install manifold3d trimesh numpy and python build.py. It rewrites the STLs in stl/ and the two preview models.
